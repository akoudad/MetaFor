# Using MetaFor

MetaFor lets you explore an answer without losing its source or your place.

## Chat

Ask a question, then select a passage and choose **Ask about this** to write a
focused follow-up beside it. Follow connected topics and use **Back** to return.
The conversation map lets you jump between topics.

Scroll inside an answer to read it. Scroll in empty space to move through its
hierarchy. Several child topics offer a choice; scrolling does not choose one
for you. Your reading position and drafts are retained.

Use **New chat** for another conversation and **Saved chats** to return.
Export `.ingx` files to keep a separate copy or transfer a conversation.

## Help on Mac

Press **Option + M** while using another app. Ask for an explanation, translation,
or visual pointer. Help shares a screen snapshot with your question and needs
Screen Recording permission. You can continue the exchange in Chat.

AI features need your own API key and an internet connection. Saved conversations
remain readable without a key. Conversations save locally; there is no cloud sync.
Topic grouping and answers depend on the model, and context can be shortened.
