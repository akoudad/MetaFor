# MetaFor for Mac

[Download MetaFor](https://metafor.pages.dev/download) · [Watch the demo](https://youtu.be/hBM_tifGJJk)

Requires **Apple silicon and macOS 26+**. AI answers need internet access and
your own model API key. The installer does not require Node.js or a terminal.

## Install and connect

1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag **Metafor** into **Applications**.
2. Open the app and choose **Add API key**. Enter your key in Settings (`⌘,`).
3. Choose **Save settings**, then **Check connection** to verify your Meta key
   and selected model. Choose **Done** to open Chat. Custom providers are checked
   when you ask your first question.

This preview is not notarized. If macOS blocks it, open **System Settings →
Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**, then reopen the app.

## Use Chat and Help

**Chat:** ask a question, follow a topic, or select a passage for a connected
follow-up. Read in order or explore the conversation map.

**Help:** press **Option + M** over the app you’re using. Allow **Screen
Recording** when asked; quit and reopen MetaFor if you change that permission
in System Settings. Ask a question to share a fresh screen snapshot with your
chosen model. Nothing records continuously. Press **Option + M** again to hide
Help, or choose **Continue in Chat** to explore further.

## Your saved chats

Chats and Help history save locally. Reinstalling preserves them; a new Mac
profile starts empty. Choose **Browse saved chats** to read existing conversations
without an API key. New AI answers still need a key.
