02 / HELP

MetaFor Help

Ask about what’s on your screen.

Bring up Help+

Press ⌥ M (Option + M) in any app to open the small Help bar. Press it again to close.

Get an answer in context+

Help shares a screen snapshot with your question to get an explanation, a translation, or a visual pointer. Screen Recording permission is required.

Continue in Chat+

Open your Help conversation in MetaFor Chat when you want to explore it further.
